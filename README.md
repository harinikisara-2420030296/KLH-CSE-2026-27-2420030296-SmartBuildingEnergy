Abstract:

The increasing demand for energy and rapid development of smart buildings have made efficient energy management an important research area.
This project proposes an Intelligent Energy Consumption Analytics framework by integrating:
Data Preprocessing → EDA → Feature Engineering → Machine Learning → Model Evaluation → Visualization.
Three benchmark datasets are considered:
Energy Efficiency Dataset
Appliances Energy Prediction Dataset
ASHRAE Great Energy Predictor III Dataset
The project applies machine learning models including:
Linear Regression,Decision Tree,Random Forest,XGBoost,CatBoost
The models are evaluated using MAE, MSE, RMSE and R² Score to identify the most suitable model for energy consumption prediction.


Objectives:

1. Analyze energy consumption patterns in smart buildings.
2. Identify peak and abnormal energy usage.
3. Visualize energy consumption trends.
4. Apply data analytics and machine learning techniques for prediction.
5. Provide insights for improving energy efficiency.
6. Support better energy management and decision-making.
